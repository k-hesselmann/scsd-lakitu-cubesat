\section{SCV Field Layout}

Full byte-level layout of the \texttt{SCV\_t} structure, referenced from
Section~\ref{sec:fsw-persistence}. The structure is packed (no padding),
so its 35-byte size is exactly the sum of its fields; that is 2 bytes
larger than earlier figures quoted elsewhere in this report, which predate
\texttt{equipment\_manual\_disable} being added to the struct.
